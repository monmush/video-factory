# Video generation — as built, updated 20 November 2026

Owns how an approved script becomes a video: the plan (recipe, shot list, build), the paid steps, what the Video tab shows meanwhile, and the stop, resume and never-pay-twice rules. What happens before committing: [scripts to videos](VIDEO_COMMIT_DECISIONS.md). Directing rules: [directing rules](DIRECTING_RULES.md). Code: `src/shared/video-job.ts` (pure job logic, tested), `src/shared/recipe.ts`, `shot-list.ts`, `build-direction.ts`, `src/main/video/` (director, runner, provider steps, FFmpeg), `src/renderer/src/components/videos.tsx`.

**Status.** Built 12–19 November. Two videos were finished live on 13 November (the first on Kling Avatar v2 Standard for $2.61; the second with the directing pass and two takes); later live runs were partial. Everything added since 16 November (recipe, shot list, build, Quality levels, flexible direction, restart safety, no cap on clips, the Cao cấp level, background sound and loudness) has run in fixture mode only. Real sound quality and the lip sync of Cao cấp's speaking model are untested. No video has been accepted by the pilot client, and repeatable economics are not validated.

## The plan: recipe, shot list, build

A plan is made per video, before any paid step, in three parts split by how often each changes.

| Part | Made | By | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Recipe** | per Quality level (built in; not yet per series) | the app (`src/shared/recipe.ts`) | which model plays each role (stills, speaking shots, motion shots), its request template, its price and limits |
| 2. **Shot list** | per video, free, about a minute | the user's own AI, no tools | the pictures (a full English description each) and the shots: words heard, who is on screen, the picture it starts from, a route, what moves, and a Vietnamese description for the operator |
| 3. **Build** | per video, instant, free | code only | the exact requests, the reference photos, the fixed sentences and the price |

Each question has one owner: the recipe chose the model, the director chose the shot, code wrote the request. The same shot list and recipe always give the same requests.

- **Routes** per shot: `speak` (the picture speaks the shot's words in the app's own voice, lip-synced), `motion` (the picture is animated without speech), `still-move` (slow push-in made locally), `product` (the product's cut-out on a generated plate; [directing rules](DIRECTING_RULES.md), "Product shots").
- **Built-in recipe:** the default image model for stills, the level's speaking model for speaking shots and, from "Cân bằng" up, Wan 3.0 Prime for motion shots. It is the only recipe; there is no second planner. `HYAN_RECIPE=<file>` (a saved recipe or live plan) replaces it in development.
- **Quality** ("Chất lượng"; offered since 19 November: Nhanh, Cân bằng (default) and Cao cấp; older levels read as the one that makes the same video) sets the output size (720p, 1080p), whether wide, detail and listener shots really move or are stills with a push-in, the speaking model and whether the video has background sound. Nhanh: stills with a push-in, Kling Avatar v2 Standard, no background sound. Cân bằng: real motion, Kling Avatar v2 Standard, background sound. Cao cấp: as Cân bằng with OmniHuman 2.0 for speaking shots (closer lip sync), a provisional pick never tested for lip sync; it is the `speaker` of the level in `src/shared/media-level.ts`, a one-line swap. Every price, the live plan's speaking route, the minimum clip length (OmniHuman bills at least 4 s, so 2–3 s speaking shots are padded and priced at 4 s) and the fixed path's model follow the level; a saved job and a saved series keep the level, and so the model, they were made with. Staff choose Quality and nothing else; model and provider names never appear in the interface. No price is shown per level (since 21 November 2026): a video is priced from its own staging, with the rates in `src/shared/cost/rates.ts`. Measured on 20 November from the provider's billed units: a moving clip at 1080p is billed 5.6 units per second and a speaking clip made by the video model at 720p with sound 2 units per second (the code had assumed 1), a picture about $0.07 (the code had assumed $0.12); the lip-sync models and the cut-out report no units, so their prices stay list prices.
- **Build** chooses the reference photos, writes the photo legend and the fixed sentences (`src/shared/prompt-text.ts`), fills the templates, checks every request against the recipe's schema and refuses a picture no shot starts from (it would be paid for).
- **Files in a video's folder:** `director-prompt.md`, `shot-list-prompt.md`, `shot-list-answer-N.json` (each full plan, numbered by call), `shot-list-correction-N.json` and `shot-list-merged-N.json` (a correction and the plan it made), `shot-list-problems-N.json` (what call N broke, repaired or failed on, and how long it took; written only when something happened, or `stopped` when the script changed during the call), `shot-list-partial-request-N.md` and `shot-list-partial-N.json` (a partial restage's request and answer), `shot-list-restage.json` (after a restage only: which path made the plan, `carried`, `partial`, `plan`, `correction`, `rewrite` or `rules`, the lines asked for and the total time), `shot-list.json`, `plan.json`, `live-plan.json`. The folder is reused when a script is staged again, so files of an earlier staging can remain; `shot-list-restage.json` and the numbers of the latest run tell them apart.
- **Staging calls (20 Nov 2026).** At most three calls (`DIRECTOR_CALLS` in `src/main/video/staging.ts`): the plan; if it breaks a rule, a **correction** that asks only for the entries that must change (pictures by id, shots by number, added or removed, whole-list fields only when a rule concerns them), merged into the first plan by `applyCorrection` (`src/shared/shot-list-correction.ts`) and checked by the same rules; if the correction is unusable or still breaks a rule, one full rewrite of the first plan as before; then the rules-only shot list. After a small script edit one partial call comes first, so a restage makes at most four (see the next two points). Never shorter plans: the director writes the same plan as before. Code also drops a picture no shot starts from when every shot names a picture that exists (it is a leftover; the 19 Nov rewrites did exactly that). Measured on the three stagings of 19 Nov: two first plans broke only that rule (now accepted with no call, identical to the rewritten plan that took 36 s and 47 s), one named a picture it never listed (a correction asking for that one picture took 2.4 s instead of the 44 s rewrite).
- **Restaging after a script edit (20 Nov 2026; built, tests only, not yet seen in the app).** When the words of a script with a valid, unpaid AI staging change, code compares the dialogue the plan was made for with the new one (`classifyScriptEdit`, `src/shared/restage.ts`). Whitespace only: the plan is kept, as before. **Small edit** (`SMALL_EDIT`, `isSmallEdit`): either lines changed in place (same speaker, same position, punctuation included) on at most 2 lines and at most a third of the script's lines, or exactly one line added or one removed with every other line identical, never the last line, and the cast and its order unchanged. Anything else (a line given to another speaker, reordered lines, more lines, a new or vanished speaker, a product change that the product patch cannot follow, a Quality level with other staging, a plan from the rules fallback or a saved shot list that does not match the plan) is a **full restage** as before. For a small edit the job keeps the old plan as its basis (`restageFrom` in `job.json`: the old lines, products, level and shot list, read from `shot-list.json` with the shots of `plan.json`, so a product patch and hand-directed descriptions are carried). Code then moves the existing plan to the new wording: shots of later lines are renumbered, a removed line's shots are dropped, and a punctuation-only change is re-cut at the same words when every part still ends at a punctuation mark. If no line is left to write, the plan is accepted with **no call** (path `carried`) when it passes every check. Otherwise one **partial** call (`shot-list-partial.md`) gets the full staging prompt with the new dialogue, the moved plan and the changed lines with their shot numbers, and answers in the correction format; `applyCorrection` merges it. The merge is refused when it changes a shot of an untouched line or a picture such a shot starts from (`untouchedChanged`), so those keep their pictures, text, routes and Vietnamese descriptions byte for byte; pictures no shot uses any more are dropped as leftovers. The merged plan goes through every check, including each line's shots reproducing its new text exactly. An unusable answer or a merged plan that breaks a rule falls back to the full staging (plan, correction, rewrite), then the rules-only list: **at most four calls** (`RESTAGE_CALLS`). A second edit before the restage ran is compared with the basis again. Replayed once by hand (a throwaway script, no AI call, not kept as a test) on three saved 19 Nov plans (11–13 lines, 14–18 shots) with a one-line edit and a hand-written answer: the merged plans passed every check and every untouched shot was identical.
- **Staging waits for the session chat and drops stale stagings (20 Nov 2026; built, tests only, not yet seen in the app).** Before: an edit that arrived while its script was being staged was ignored until the staging finished (all its calls, each up to 240 s, holding one of the five Claude processes); the finished plan fitted the old words, so the script was then staged again in full. During a chat turn, staging of an edited script started after any 3-second pause in board writes, so a later edit in the same turn wasted it. Now: a staging whose script's words change or which is deleted is **stopped** (its Claude process killed, or its place in the queue given up) and its result is never applied; the newest words are then staged once (partially when small). While a chat turn runs, staging of every script the turn touched waits until the turn ends, fails or is stopped (`holdStaging`; at most 15 minutes, `MAX_STAGING_HOLD_MS`; holds live only in memory, so a restart releases them), then starts on the final text after the usual 3 s. Manual edits keep the 3 s delay. A waiting or running script shows "Đang lên dàn dựng".
- **A product change patches the plan in code** (product shots show the new product, the plate is kept, the cut-out price follows) with no new staging when the words are unchanged or only the product's name was swapped; otherwise the script is staged again. A script counts as done only when its dialogue and its staging both exist. Staging runs by itself only when the text AI is the user's own.
- **Changing Quality after scripts exist** stages every unpaid script again without asking (paid videos keep their plan), except between Cân bằng and Cao cấp, whose staging is the same: there only the requests are rebuilt in code. This is a known design problem; the redesign is undecided.
- **Development switches** (fixture mode): `HYAN_FIXTURE_SHOT_LIST=<answer.json>` runs a saved shot-list answer through the checks; `HYAN_FIXTURE_LIVE_PLAN=<folder>` replays a saved plan. `HYAN_FIXTURE_CAPTIONS=4x1,12x3` makes clip 4 come back with added lettering once and clip 12 three times (whatever model made it, so clip 4's redo is on the other speaking model and clip 12 fails after both). `HYAN_FIXTURE_SOUND_FAIL=ambience,spot:4x1` makes the ambience fail every attempt and shot 5's spot sound fail once. Fixture sounds are real local audio: brown noise for the ambience, a fading 3 kHz tone for a spot sound, so the mix can be heard and measured.

Not built: a recipe made by an AI session with read-only planning tools (per series, with pinning and standby models), planning by itself after approval, and one executor for the fixed and the planned path (today both exist and duplicate pricing, state and request building).

## Pipeline, cheap steps first

1. **Shot plan** (free): the plan above.
2. **Voice** (cents): one multi-speaker dialogue request for the whole script, each line in its character's voice with the approved dry close-mic direction ([voices](VOICE_DECISIONS.md)). Line and character timings place every shot on the dialogue's timeline; speaking shots get their stretch of audio, listeners get silence. The provider's full timing is saved beside the audio as `*.timing.json`. A line may end with a sound (`[cười]`), sent as the voice model's own tag. **Holds:** the video's timeline is the dialogue's with each hold's silence added after its line; a held speaking shot is sent its words plus the silence. The silence is inserted locally (`withHolds`, `holdInserts` in `src/shared/shot-plan.ts`, `insertSilence` in `ffmpeg.ts`), with no new voice request.
3. **Stills** (cents each): the master picture first, then every other picture drawn from it with the characters' reference photos. A product shot is the product's cut-out on a generated plate. Each generated still is checked by the user's own AI and remade once if it is wrong.
4. **Shots** (almost all the cost): speaking shots by the speaking model, `motion` shots by the motion model. **Every clip that has what it needs is sent at once** (since 19 November; before, at most three; fal queues what is over the account's concurrency limit). One video still runs at a time and local shots one at a time. Shots without motion are the still with a slow push-in, made locally for free; a product shot moves its plate and its product at different rates. **Every returned clip is checked** (since 19 November, after two Kling clips came back with invented Chinese captions): three frames (start, middle, end) are compared by the user's own AI with the still the clip was made from, and a clip with added lettering is set aside (`shot-NN-rejected-K.mp4`) and the shot sent again, silently. At most 2 automatic redos per shot and 4 per video (`src/shared/clip-check.ts`, counts in `job.json`); past that the shot fails like any other and the user redoes it. **A speaking clip with added lettering is made next on another speaking model** (since 20 November, after one shot on the default model got a Chinese sticker five times out of five): same picture and audio, only that shot. The shot keeps the models that were rejected for it (`rejectedSpeakers` in `job.json`) and takes the first untried one from a short ordered list (`src/shared/speaker-switch.ts`: the default model, then OmniHuman 2.0; a Cao cấp video therefore falls back to the default model). On a planned video the shot's route in the plan is rewritten to the new model, so its input shape, 4-second minimum, price, cost record and reattachment after a restart follow. When every model on the list has been rejected the shot fails at once rather than paying for the same model again; a manual retry still sends it once more. A shot that failed this way before the record existed counts its own model as rejected when the job is loaded. A version at another Quality level avoids a model rejected for that shot and reuses a clip another version already made with the replacement. Moving shots keep their model and are only redone within the limits. A check that cannot run is tried once more and the clip is then accepted, so a broken checker never spends. A set-aside clip's cost moves to "Làm lại". Prompt-only video models are no longer told "no captions"; they are told the frame is clean, unedited footage like the reference image (`CLEAN_FOOTAGE`).
5. **Background sound** (Cân bằng and Cao cấp, a few cents): the shot list adds one English ambience for the place (`ambience`) and, on a few shots that do not speak, an English sound of the action (`sound`); never on a speaker shot, never music or voices (dropped by code if written). Each description is at most 16 words; a longer one is cut at the last comma-separated part that fits (since 23 November: before, it was dropped whole, which left one live video without any room sound). Low-level plans and old plans have neither and stay valid. After the voice, code turns them into sound steps in `job.json` (`sounds`): the ambience as one request of up to 30 s made to loop, each spot sound as long as its shot. They are sent alongside stills and clips, marked before sending and attached after a restart like them, priced before the hold, counted in spent figures and logged in `usage.jsonl`. A sound that fails or passes its 10-minute deadline is tried once more, then dropped; the video is assembled without it, and a sound that cannot be mixed is left out. **The sound source is swappable:** `src/shared/sound.ts` has one interface between what the plan asks for (cues: role, text, start, length) and what the mixer gets (audio layers: a file or a clip's own audio track, start, length, role); `SOUND_SOURCE` picks the source in one place and its maker lives in `src/main/video/sounds.ts`. Today's source is generated effects from text (`elevenlabs/sound-effects/v3` on fal, $0.002/s). A source that keeps a clip's own sound would return clip layers and no requests, with no change to the planner, the runner's steps or the mixer. The Dàn dựng tab marks a shot with a spot sound "Tiếng động".
6. **Assembly** (free, local): each clip cut to its exact place, joined, the whole dialogue laid underneath (with the holds' silence), subtitles burned in. **Mix:** each layer is measured and set relative to the voice (voice levelled to −16 LUFS; ambience 22 dB below it, looped when shorter than the video, faded in over 1 s and out over 1.5 s; spot sounds 12 dB below, starting at their shot and cut at its end), the bed is ducked under the voice, and the final mix is normalised in two passes to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.5 dB, at every level. Earlier videos measured about −21.5 LUFS. The soundtrack is padded and trimmed to the video's exact length, so subtitles and timing do not move. Short-form style (`src/shared/subtitles.ts`): a phrase of up to five words, bold white with a black outline, centred below the faces; the word being spoken turns yellow and grows slightly, alone. Phrases split at sentence ends, at commas once a phrase has three words, then into balanced parts. Words are timed from the voice's saved character timing; without it, spread across each line in proportion to length. A phrase never runs into the next line. Needs `ffmpeg` installed; the job refuses before the voice step if it is missing, so nothing is paid for.

**Lip sync.** Measured on the first live video: the provider keeps our sound (within 30 ms), assembly does not drift (within ±40 ms of its cut), and the voice runs at a normal 3.7 syllables a second. The mouth itself runs late on some faces; a 150 ms shift was tried and removed on 13 November so each speaking model can be judged unshifted. Re-measure per model before adding any shift back.

Jobs saved before plans existed are read as one speaker shot per line.

The first paid step of a planned video stops at a pause after voice and stills ("Nghe và xem cảnh chính"). Its hold is labelled "Giữ để tạo các cảnh chính" and priced to the pause; the second hold ("Giữ để tạo video") makes the rest. A finished video can be fixed in place, paying only for what changes: [reviewing and fixing videos](VIDEO_REVIEW_DECISIONS.md). Files made by a fix carry its revision (`shot-07-r2.mp4`, `final-r2.mp4`). Files live in the session's media folder under `videos/<scriptId>/`.

## Speaking-shot models

The registry `src/shared/video-models.ts` (pure, tested) lists each model's id, level, name and maker, fal endpoint, what it makes and costs at each output size, its audio limits and whether it takes a prompt. How each endpoint is asked and answered lives in `src/main/video/shot-models.ts`. Prices are snapshots read from fal's public pages on 13 November. The pipeline uses only the recipe's model; the others are kept for reading old jobs. Model names are for developers; they never appear in the interface.

| Level | Model (maker) | Output at 720p / 1080p | $/s at 720p / 1080p | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Kling Avatar v2 Standard (Kuaishou), recipe default | 720p | 0.0562 | none listed |
| Standard | MiniMax H3 Max Lip Sync (MiniMax) | 768p / 1080p | 0.08 / 0.16 | audio at least 5 s; 1.2× rate over 15 s |
| Premium | OmniHuman 2.0 (ByteDance), provisional | 720p | 0.105 | audio 4–300 s |
| Premium | Kling Avatar v2 Pro (Kuaishou) | 1080p | 0.115 | none listed |
| Premium | VEED Fabric 1.0 (VEED) | 720p | 0.15 | none listed |

Run live so far: Kling Standard, VEED Fabric 1.0 and Kling Avatar v2 Pro. MiniMax H3 and OmniHuman 2.0 have not. Only Kling Avatar v2 Standard and Pro and OmniHuman 2.0 take a prompt, so only they hear the seat's ambient motion ([directing rules](DIRECTING_RULES.md), B8).

Rules that still hold:

- One speaking model per video, fixed when its first shot is sent and saved with the job before anything goes out, so a restart polls the right endpoint. Each shot records the model and request id the moment it is sent; polling always uses that endpoint.
- A shot longer than its model takes is made by the default model.
- Audio shorter than a model's minimum is padded with silence before sending and billed at the minimum; assembly trims the clip. Estimates add that minimum per shot.
- Every estimate, button price and job cost uses the shot's model (`shotModel`, `shotEstimateUsd`); `usage.jsonl` and `requests.jsonl` carry the model id.
- Images sent to fal are downscaled to 1536 px on the long side before upload. Every local step follows the video's output size, subtitles included, proportionally.

## Rules

- **Hold to spend.** Every paid action in the interface is a 3-second hold that shows its price ([scripts to videos](VIDEO_COMMIT_DECISIONS.md)).- **The cost breakdown is always visible.** "Chi phí" opens before, during and after the video, with a frozen estimate ("Dự tính") beside what was paid ("Đã chi") and redone work on its own row. One price rule everywhere ([design lessons](DESIGN_LESSONS.md), "Prices").
- **Paid amounts come from the provider where it reports them.** Each finished request's billed units are read from its result (`src/main/fal.ts`) and kept as an append-only list of charges on the job (`src/shared/cost/charges.ts`); the total paid is their sum. A request whose provider reports nothing is recorded at the calculated price and shown with "~".
- **Leaving never wastes paid work silently.** Stopping a video withdraws the requests the provider has not started (free) and keeps polling the rest; quitting with paid work in flight asks first, then waits up to 10 s for work in progress and saved state; deleting a session or series cancels its requests and logs those that may still be billed; a sidebar spinner and the dock badge show videos being made.
- **Stop** (hold 1.2 s; the button shows what is lost): shots not yet sent are never sent. Shots already rendering finish, are charged and their clips kept. **"Giữ để tạo tiếp"** continues from what exists and pays only for what is missing.
- **Never pay twice, also across a restart.** Each still and clip is marked in `job.json` before it is sent, and its `requests.jsonl` line carries the mark. The request id is saved the moment the provider accepts it. On start, a request fal accepted but `job.json` never got is attached by its mark and polled, not resent (`src/shared/request-reconcile.ts`). Video cut-outs go through the queue and are picked up the same way. Paid voice and cut-out results are saved before the step is settled, so a failure or a restart reuses them. `job.json` writes are flushed before the rename, and leftover `.tmp` files are removed on start. Requests sent before the mark existed are not covered.
- **Deadlines.** A request past its deadline is cancelled at the provider and counts as one failed attempt: a still 30 minutes, a clip 90, a cut-out 20, a sound 10 (`src/shared/request-deadline.ts`).
- **Failures:** one automatic retry per shot, then "Thử lại" on that shot. A provider-side failure is not logged as a cost. Failed downloads and polls count as attempts instead of looping.
- **Network trouble is not a failure** (`src/shared/net-error.ts`): a lost connection, DNS, timeouts and HTTP 408, 429 and 5xx leave the step as it was, with saved request ids kept and polled. The job stays running and marked offline, retries after 5, 10, 20 and 40 seconds and then every minute, and the card says "Mất kết nối, sẽ thử lại" with "Thử lại" to try now. `HYAN_FIXTURE_OFFLINE=<seconds>` simulates an outage.
- **Subtitles go on last** and clips are kept without them, so restyling later is free.
- Scripts committed before jobs existed show as not started; nothing starts by itself on upgrade.

## Video tab

A card per script. What it is sits on the left, what can be done on the right: the title, and under it one muted line with the status and the cost; top right the one button that applies, and under it the quiet actions ("Bản trước", "Sửa kịch bản", "Sửa video"). The cost is not beside the button, where it read as that button's price. "▶ Lời thoại" appears when the voice is ready. While a video is being staged the card says "Đang lên dàn dựng". Finished: the player beside its shots, and "Tải xuống". Every word is a state, a number or an action; no guidance text.

**Time per step (23 November 2026; built, not yet seen in the app).** Each job keeps the seconds of work per phase (`phaseSec`: plan, voice, stills, speech, shots, assembly), counted only while the job is running: waiting for another video, the pause, stopped, failed and the app being closed add nothing (`src/shared/phase-clock.ts`, applied wherever the runner saves a job). While a video is made each step shows its time and the current one counts up, and the status line leads with the total ("2:10 · còn ~3 phút"). A finished video shows a clock with the total; hovering it lists the steps. A fix or another quality version adds to the same totals. The staging done before "Tạo video" is not counted, and videos made earlier show nothing.

**A picture full size (23 November 2026; built, not yet seen in the app).** Every shot tile with a picture has "Xem ảnh" on hover or focus; it opens the video's pictures in the full-size viewer (← →, zoom), each shown once and labelled with the shots that use it.

## Native voice (20 November 2026)

**Status: built, fixture/tests only, never run live.** No request has been sent to any of the three models below; their lip sync, Vietnamese pronunciation, accent and loudness are unknown.

A second way to make the speaking shots, behind a developer setting (Settings, "Thử nghiệm", "Video tự tạo giọng nói"; shown only in development or with `HYAN_DEV=1`, saved as `nativeVoice` in `settings.json`, off by default). With it on there is no voice step: a video model that makes its own speech turns each speaking still and a text prompt into a clip, and the dialogue track and the timeline are taken from those clips. Code: `src/shared/speech.ts` (segments, lengths, speech detection, timeline), `src/shared/speech-models.ts` (models), `speechPrompt` in `src/shared/prompt-text.ts`, `voiceDescription` in `src/shared/voices.ts`, `src/main/video/speech.ts` (requests), the native branches in `src/shared/video-job.ts` and `src/main/video/runner.ts`.

- **The mode is taken once.** A job records `voiceMode: "native"` when it is made; until it starts (still idle, nothing paid, no segment sent) it follows the setting, and the hold re-reads it. After that it never changes. A job without the field is dubbed, so every older job and every job made with the setting off behaves, prices and asks exactly as before. Staging is the same in both modes; switching the setting never stages again.
- **Speech segments** (pure, `speechSegments`): each `speaker` shot starts a segment; the other shots of the line (wide, detail, listener, product, motion) belong to the segment before them, or to the first one when they come before any speaker shot. A line without a speaker shot gets one segment from a picture of its speaker (else the master), whose video is never shown. A segment's text is its shots' words joined; its hold is its last shot's.
- **Models per Quality level** (`speech` in `src/shared/media-level.ts`): Nhanh MiniMax H3 Max ($0.0375/s, 1–15 s whole seconds; output may run ~0.7 s long), Cân bằng Wan 3.0 ($0.09/s as billed on 21 Nov, two $0.045 units per second with sound; 2–30 s), Cao cấp Veo 3.1 Fast ($0.15/s, 4, 6 or 8 s). Prices read on 20 Nov 2026. A segment asks for its estimated speaking time (the app's syllable estimate, plus 1 s for a sound at the end of a line) plus its hold plus 1 s, rounded up to what the model takes; estimates and costs use that rounded length. A segment too long for the level's model goes to the first model in the list (the level's, then Wan 3.0, Veo 3.1 Fast, H3 Max) that takes it.
- **Prompt** (code only): who speaks (the main person when the picture has several; the others silent and still), the motion the plan wrote for the shot, `They say: "<words>"` with a sound at the end of the line removed from the quote and described after it ("then they laugh softly"), one fixed English voice sentence per gender and age band (from `guessVoiceTraits`, with "standard Northern Vietnamese (Hanoi) accent"), the seat's background motion, "the natural quiet sound of the room; no music", faithfulness, and `CLEAN_FOOTAGE`.
- **Segments are steps like sounds and clips:** in `job.json` (`segments`), marked before sending and the mark in `requests.jsonl` (purpose `video-speech`, with model and length), request id saved on acceptance, attached after a restart, polled, 90-minute deadline, one automatic retry then "Thử lại" on the segment's speaker shot, network trouble handled as for clips, stop and resume as for clips, cost recorded in `usage.jsonl`. Every segment whose still exists is sent at once.
- **Each returned clip** goes through the lettering check with the same limits; a rejected one is set aside (`speech-<id>-rejected-N.mp4`) and redone on the next model in the list above, and the segment fails when every model was rejected. Speech is then found with FFmpeg `silencedetect` (−35 dB, 0.3 s); a clip whose speech runs to its very end is set aside (`speech-<id>-cut-N.mp4`) as one failed attempt and asked again longer (next allowed length, or 2 s more). A set-aside clip's cost moves to "Làm lại".
- **Timeline:** when every segment is done, each segment's speech is cut with 0.15 s before and after, levelled to −16 LUFS, and joined in line order into the job's dialogue file; `lineSpans`, `dialogueSec` and a per-segment character timing (used only to split shots, so a cut between two segments falls between their words) come from the joined track. The holds are not in that file: they are added after it exactly as for a dubbed video (`withHolds`, `insertSilence`), so they are counted once; the clip was asked long enough to cover its hold. Then the existing placement, listener silence, motion audio, sounds, mix, subtitles (spread across each line by length, as without voice timing) and assembly run unchanged. Speaker shots are local steps: cut from their segment's clip at their offset in it (frozen first or last frame if the shot reaches past the clip), scaled to the output size, no cost of their own.
- **Order:** plan, stills, speech segments, timeline, the other shots and sounds, assembly. The first hold of a planned video stops after the stills ("Xem cảnh chính"), priced without a voice; "▶ Lời thoại" appears once the dialogue file exists. A native job never calls the voice code, needs no ElevenLabs key and is not warned about shared voices.
- **Prices and interface:** estimates on the hold and in the cost panel follow the setting for videos not started; the cost table has no voice row and one "Cảnh nói (có tiếng)" group with a "Nói" row per segment (since 25 Nov; it was "Lời nói", which read as the price of sound; the normal mode's "Khẩu hình" became "Cảnh nói", and estimates saved under the old names are shown under the new ones, `src/shared/cost/group-names.ts`); paid and in-flight figures include segments. Everything the cost code knows about this mode is in `src/shared/cost/speech-cost.ts`, registered by one line in `src/shared/cost/parts.ts`. The card shows "Tạo lời nói" with a count while segments run.
- **Fixes:** remaking a still that no segment starts from, a wide, detail or product shot, or a moving or listening shot's clip works as before. Anything that would change the speech is refused once speech exists ("Video tự tạo giọng nói chưa sửa được phần lời nói"): remaking a speaker shot or a segment's still, replacing a line, redoing the voice; such tiles cannot be picked. "Thử chất lượng khác" is not offered for a native video.
- **Fixture mode:** a segment's clip is the still with a stretch of the fixture dialogue starting at 0.4 s and lasting the estimated speaking time, in a clip of the asked length. `HYAN_FIXTURE_CUTOFF=3x1` makes the segment whose speaker shot is shot 3 run its speech to the end once; `HYAN_FIXTURE_FAIL` and `HYAN_FIXTURE_CAPTIONS` apply to a segment by its speaker shot's number.

Not built: music; a per-character voice text field (the voice sentence comes from gender and age only); remaking a speaker shot, a segment, a line or the voice on a native video; versions at another Quality level of a native video; the H3 Max overrun is not measured or trimmed beyond what speech detection does; the estimate before a plan still counts generated pictures for a series without a place.

## Fixture mode

`pnpm dev` simulates every provider step in a few seconds with sample media, so the whole flow can be driven for free. `HYAN_FIXTURE_FAIL="2,4x2"` makes shot 2 fail once and shot 4 twice. Real requests need `HYAN_PROVIDER_MODE=live`.

## Known risks

- The instant between fal accepting a request and the app logging it is not covered: a lost reply could still be paid twice. Rare; check the usage log after live runs.
- Whether a failed shot is billed by the provider is unverified; the app assumes not.
- Very short lines (one or two words) may be shorter than the speaking model accepts. Untested.
- The first live run with no cap on clips is still to be watched: total time from the first clip to assembly, and any clip that stays rendering for long.

## Not built

**Sound beyond room tone (found in the second user test, 21 November 2026; nothing decided).** Today a video gets one room sound, mixed 22 dB under the voice, and a spot sound only on shots where nobody speaks: the two test videos had one spot sound each. There is no sound under a speaking shot, none at a cut, none for the mood of a moment, and no music, by rule. Candidates, each needing a listening test: a louder room sound, action sounds under speaking shots, short sounds at scene changes and at a turn in mood, a music bed per series (needs a source and a decision on rights).

Several videos in parallel; generation while the app is closed; bundling FFmpeg.

**Caption styles (later, a likely differentiator):** a set of styles with size and position, chosen per series, building on today's word-by-word highlight. Drawn by the app itself, not a provider's caption service. Start by testing fonts against hard Vietnamese syllables (stacked accents such as "ễ", "ặ"): most display fonts used by caption tools do not cover them.
