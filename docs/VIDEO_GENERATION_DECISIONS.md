# Video generation — as built, updated 25 November 2026

Owns how an approved script becomes a video: the plan (recipe, shot list, build), the paid steps, what the Video tab shows meanwhile, and the stop, resume and never-pay-twice rules. What happens before committing: [scripts to videos](VIDEO_COMMIT_DECISIONS.md). Directing rules: [directing rules](DIRECTING_RULES.md). Code: `src/shared/video-job.ts` (pure job logic, tested), `src/shared/recipe.ts`, `shot-list.ts`, `build-direction.ts`, `src/main/video/` (director, runner, provider steps, FFmpeg), `src/renderer/src/components/videos.tsx`.

**25 November 2026: one way of making videos.** The video model speaks every line (section "The video model speaks" below, formerly "Native voice", is the only path); the separate voice step, lip-sync models and Quality levels are removed, and a series names its picture and video model ([scripts to videos](VIDEO_COMMIT_DECISIONS.md), "Models, named"). Earlier history below (the status paragraph, measurements) refers to the old way.

**Status.** Built 12–19 November. Two videos were finished live on 13 November (the first on Kling Avatar v2 Standard for $2.61; the second with the directing pass and two takes); later live runs were partial. Everything added since 16 November (recipe, shot list, build, Quality levels, flexible direction, restart safety, no cap on clips, the Cao cấp level, background sound and loudness) has run in fixture mode only. Real sound quality and the lip sync of Cao cấp's speaking model are untested. No video has been accepted by the pilot client, and repeatable economics are not validated.

## The plan: recipe, shot list, build

A plan is made per video, before any paid step, in three parts split by how often each changes.

| Part | Made | By | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Recipe** | per series models | the app (`src/shared/recipe.ts`) | the picture model for stills and the video model for moving shots, their request templates, prices and limits |
| 2. **Shot list** | per video, free, about a minute | the user's own AI, no tools | the pictures (a full English description each) and the shots: words heard, who is on screen, the picture it starts from, a route, what moves, and a Vietnamese description for the operator |
| 3. **Build** | per video, instant, free | code only | the exact requests, the reference photos, the fixed sentences and the price |

Each question has one owner: the recipe chose the model, the director chose the shot, code wrote the request. The same shot list and recipe always give the same requests.

- **Routes** per shot: `speak` (stored as `lip-sync`: the shot is cut from its speech segment, made by the video model; no request of its own), `motion` (the video model animates the picture without sound; a listener the director asked to `speak` becomes `motion` with a listening prompt), `still-move` (slow push-in made locally), `product` (the product's cut-out on a generated plate; [directing rules](DIRECTING_RULES.md), "Product shots").
- **Built-in recipe** from the job's models (`builtinRecipe(models)`). Requests are built at send time from the job's models (`submitLiveFrame`, `submitMotion` in `src/main/video/steps.ts`), so a model changed after staging needs no new staging; the plan's stored endpoints only record what the series had when it was staged. `HYAN_RECIPE` is removed.
- **Fixed for every video (25 November 2026):** 720p output, real motion on shots where nobody speaks, background sound, and the director's preference "Shots that do not speak really move". The Quality levels (Nhanh, Cân bằng, Cao cấp) are removed. Measured on 20 November from billed units: a speaking clip by Wan 3.0 at 720p with sound is 2 units per second, a GPT Image still about $0.07. Unmeasured, assumed 1 unit per second and named so in `src/shared/cost/rates.ts`: Wan 3.0 silent motion and MiniMax H3 Max; Nano Banana Pro is assumed 1 unit per still. Real billed units are read from every result.
- **Build** chooses the reference photos, writes the photo legend and the fixed sentences (`src/shared/prompt-text.ts`), fills the templates, checks every request against the recipe's schema and refuses a picture no shot starts from (it would be paid for).
- **Files in a video's folder:** `director-prompt.md`, `shot-list-prompt.md`, `shot-list-answer-N.json` (each full plan, numbered by call), `shot-list-correction-N.json` and `shot-list-merged-N.json` (a correction and the plan it made), `shot-list-problems-N.json` (what call N broke, repaired or failed on, and how long it took; written only when something happened, or `stopped` when the script changed during the call), `shot-list-partial-request-N.md` and `shot-list-partial-N.json` (a partial restage's request and answer), `shot-list-restage.json` (after a restage only: which path made the plan, `carried`, `partial`, `plan`, `correction`, `rewrite` or `rules`, the lines asked for and the total time), `shot-list.json`, `plan.json`, `live-plan.json`. The folder is reused when a script is staged again, so files of an earlier staging can remain; `shot-list-restage.json` and the numbers of the latest run tell them apart.
- **Staging calls (20 Nov 2026).** At most three calls (`DIRECTOR_CALLS` in `src/main/video/staging.ts`): the plan; if it breaks a rule, a **correction** that asks only for the entries that must change (pictures by id, shots by number, added or removed, whole-list fields only when a rule concerns them), merged into the first plan by `applyCorrection` (`src/shared/shot-list-correction.ts`) and checked by the same rules; if the correction is unusable or still breaks a rule, one full rewrite of the first plan as before; then the rules-only shot list. After a small script edit one partial call comes first, so a restage makes at most four (see the next two points). Never shorter plans: the director writes the same plan as before. Code also drops a picture no shot starts from when every shot names a picture that exists (it is a leftover; the 19 Nov rewrites did exactly that). Measured on the three stagings of 19 Nov: two first plans broke only that rule (now accepted with no call, identical to the rewritten plan that took 36 s and 47 s), one named a picture it never listed (a correction asking for that one picture took 2.4 s instead of the 44 s rewrite).
- **Restaging after a script edit (20 Nov 2026; built, tests only, not yet seen in the app).** When the words of a script with a valid, unpaid AI staging change, code compares the dialogue the plan was made for with the new one (`classifyScriptEdit`, `src/shared/restage.ts`). Whitespace only: the plan is kept, as before. **Small edit** (`SMALL_EDIT`, `isSmallEdit`): either lines changed in place (same speaker, same position, punctuation included) on at most 2 lines and at most a third of the script's lines, or exactly one line added or one removed with every other line identical, never the last line, and the cast and its order unchanged. Anything else (a line given to another speaker, reordered lines, more lines, a new or vanished speaker, a product change that the product patch cannot follow, a plan from the rules fallback or a saved shot list that does not match the plan) is a **full restage** as before. For a small edit the job keeps the old plan as its basis (`restageFrom` in `job.json`: the old lines, products and shot list, read from `shot-list.json` with the shots of `plan.json`, so a product patch and hand-directed descriptions are carried). Code then moves the existing plan to the new wording: shots of later lines are renumbered, a removed line's shots are dropped, and a punctuation-only change is re-cut at the same words when every part still ends at a punctuation mark. If no line is left to write, the plan is accepted with **no call** (path `carried`) when it passes every check. Otherwise one **partial** call (`shot-list-partial.md`) gets the full staging prompt with the new dialogue, the moved plan and the changed lines with their shot numbers, and answers in the correction format; `applyCorrection` merges it. The merge is refused when it changes a shot of an untouched line or a picture such a shot starts from (`untouchedChanged`), so those keep their pictures, text, routes and Vietnamese descriptions byte for byte; pictures no shot uses any more are dropped as leftovers. The merged plan goes through every check, including each line's shots reproducing its new text exactly. An unusable answer or a merged plan that breaks a rule falls back to the full staging (plan, correction, rewrite), then the rules-only list: **at most four calls** (`RESTAGE_CALLS`). A second edit before the restage ran is compared with the basis again. Replayed once by hand (a throwaway script, no AI call, not kept as a test) on three saved 19 Nov plans (11–13 lines, 14–18 shots) with a one-line edit and a hand-written answer: the merged plans passed every check and every untouched shot was identical.
- **Staging waits for the session chat and drops stale stagings (20 Nov 2026; built, tests only, not yet seen in the app).** Before: an edit that arrived while its script was being staged was ignored until the staging finished (all its calls, each up to 240 s, holding one of the five Claude processes); the finished plan fitted the old words, so the script was then staged again in full. During a chat turn, staging of an edited script started after any 3-second pause in board writes, so a later edit in the same turn wasted it. Now: a staging whose script's words change or which is deleted is **stopped** (its Claude process killed, or its place in the queue given up) and its result is never applied; the newest words are then staged once (partially when small). While a chat turn runs, staging of every script the turn touched waits until the turn ends, fails or is stopped (`holdStaging`; at most 15 minutes, `MAX_STAGING_HOLD_MS`; holds live only in memory, so a restart releases them), then starts on the final text after the usual 3 s. Manual edits keep the 3 s delay. A waiting or running script shows "Đang lên dàn dựng".
- **A product change patches the plan in code** (product shots show the new product, the plate is kept, the cut-out price follows) with no new staging when the words are unchanged or only the product's name was swapped; otherwise the script is staged again. A script counts as done only when its dialogue and its staging both exist. Staging runs by itself only when the text AI is the user's own.
- **Development switches** (fixture mode): `HYAN_FIXTURE_SHOT_LIST=<answer.json>` runs a saved shot-list answer through the checks; `HYAN_FIXTURE_LIVE_PLAN=<folder>` replays a saved plan. `HYAN_FIXTURE_CAPTIONS=4x1,12x3` makes clip 4 come back with added lettering once and clip 12 three times. `HYAN_FIXTURE_SOUND_FAIL=ambience,spot:4x1` makes the ambience fail every attempt and shot 5's spot sound fail once. Fixture sounds are real local audio: brown noise for the ambience, a fading 3 kHz tone for a spot sound, so the mix can be heard and measured.

Not built: a recipe made by an AI session with read-only planning tools (per series, with pinning and standby models), planning by itself after approval, and one executor for the fixed and the planned path (today both exist and duplicate pricing, state and request building).

## Pipeline, cheap steps first

1. **Shot plan** (free): the plan above.
2. **Stills** (cents each): the master picture first, then every other picture drawn from it with the characters' reference photos, by the job's picture model. A product shot is the product's cut-out on a generated plate. Each generated still is checked by the user's own AI and remade once if it is wrong.
3. **Character voices** (about $0.01 each, only when missing; alongside the stills): one voice sample per speaking character, made from the character's voice text and shared by content across videos ([voices](VOICE_DECISIONS.md)).
4. **Speech** (most of the cost): one segment per speaking shot, made by the job's video model from its still, the character's voice sample and a text prompt; see "The video model speaks" below.
5. **Timeline** (free, local): the dialogue track and every shot's place are taken from the speech clips.
6. **Other shots:** `motion` shots by the job's video model (its image-to-video endpoint, starting from the still, without sound); a failed one falls back to the still with a push-in. Speaking shots are cut from their segment. Every returned clip is checked for added lettering (three frames against its still); a moving clip with lettering is sent again on the same model, at most 2 automatic redos per shot and 4 per video (`src/shared/clip-check.ts`). A rejected speech clip goes to the other video model. A check that cannot run is tried once more and the clip is then accepted.
7. **Background sound** (a few cents): unchanged; one ambience for the place and spot sounds on a few shots that do not speak (`src/shared/sound.ts`, `src/main/video/sounds.ts`, generated effects at $0.002/s).
8. **Assembly** (free, local): each clip cut to its place, joined, the dialogue laid underneath with the holds' silence, the mix levelled, subtitles burned in. Subtitle words are spread across each line by length.

The first paid step of a planned video stops at a pause after the stills ("Xem cảnh chính"); its hold is "Giữ để tạo các cảnh chính", the second "Giữ để tạo video". A finished video can be fixed in place except for its speech: [reviewing and fixing videos](VIDEO_REVIEW_DECISIONS.md). Files live in the session's media folder under `videos/<scriptId>/`.

**Jobs saved before 25 November 2026** (`migrateJob`, `src/shared/video-job.ts`, job version 3): a video made by the video model keeps working, with `models.video` from its old level (Nhanh: MiniMax H3 Max, otherwise Wan 3.0; Veo is gone) and `models.image` GPT Image 2.6. A finished video made the old way is kept to watch (`dubbed`). An unfinished one made the old way is reset to a fresh, not-started video (`oldFiles`: its folder is never deleted, new files use a new revision).

## Video models

`src/shared/video-models.ts`: each model makes speech (with sound, a reference-to-video endpoint) and motion (silent, image-to-video) from a still. Prices read on 25 November 2026, per billed unit.

| Model | Speech endpoint and request | Motion endpoint and request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wan 3.0 (Tự động) | `alibaba/wan-3.0/reference-to-video`, $0.05: `reference_image_urls: [still]`, `reference_audio_urls: [voice]`, 2–30 s, 720p, 9:16, `audio: true`; no start frame (2 units/s assumed, unmeasured) | `alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video`, $0.05: `start_image_url`, 720p, `audio: false` (1 unit/s assumed) |
| MiniMax H3 Max | `minimax/h3-max/reference-to-video`, $0.05: `image_url: still` (first frame), `reference_image_urls: [still]`, `reference_audio_urls: [voice]`, 1–15 s, 768P, 9:16, no prompt expansion (1 unit/s assumed; output may run ~0.7 s long) | `minimax/h3-max/image-to-video`, $0.0375: `image_url`, 768P (1 unit/s assumed) |

The voice sample is uploaded with the still for every speech request (never in fixture mode). A clip rejected for lettering is redone on the other model with the same voice sample.

Picture models (`src/shared/image-models.ts`): GPT Image 2.6 (Tự động) and Nano Banana Pro, both `prompt` + `image_urls` at 9:16, at most 8 references each (not rechecked against the live schemas in this change). The angle generator and place cleaning keep their own model choice.

## Rules

- **Hold to spend.** Every paid action in the interface is a 3-second hold that shows its price ([scripts to videos](VIDEO_COMMIT_DECISIONS.md)).- **The cost breakdown is always visible.** "Chi phí" opens before, during and after the video, with a frozen estimate ("Dự tính") beside what was paid ("Đã chi") and redone work on its own row. One price rule everywhere ([design lessons](DESIGN_LESSONS.md), "Prices").
- **Paid amounts come from the provider where it reports them.** Each finished request's billed units are read from its result (`src/main/fal.ts`) and kept as an append-only list of charges on the job (`src/shared/cost/charges.ts`); the total paid is their sum. A request whose provider reports nothing is recorded at the calculated price and shown with "~".
- **Leaving never wastes paid work silently.** Stopping a video withdraws the requests the provider has not started (free) and keeps polling the rest; quitting with paid work in flight asks first, then waits up to 10 s for work in progress and saved state; deleting a session or series cancels its requests and logs those that may still be billed; a sidebar spinner and the dock badge show videos being made.
- **Stop** (hold 1.2 s; the button shows what is lost): shots not yet sent are never sent. Shots already rendering finish, are charged and their clips kept. **"Giữ để tạo tiếp"** continues from what exists and pays only for what is missing.
- **Never pay twice, also across a restart.** Each still and clip is marked in `job.json` before it is sent, and its `requests.jsonl` line carries the mark. The request id is saved the moment the provider accepts it. On start, a request fal accepted but `job.json` never got is attached by its mark and polled, not resent (`src/shared/request-reconcile.ts`). Video cut-outs go through the queue and are picked up the same way. Paid voice and cut-out results are saved before the step is settled, so a failure or a restart reuses them. `job.json` writes are flushed before the rename, and leftover `.tmp` files are removed on start. Requests sent before the mark existed are not covered.
- **Deadlines.** A request past its deadline is cancelled at the provider and counts as one failed attempt: a still 30 minutes, a clip 90, a cut-out 20, a sound 10 (`src/shared/request-deadline.ts`).
- **Failures:** one automatic retry per shot, then "Thử lại" on that shot. A provider-side failure is not logged as a cost. Failed downloads and polls count as attempts instead of looping.
- **Network trouble is not a failure** (`src/shared/net-error.ts`): a lost connection, DNS, timeouts and HTTP 408, 429 and 5xx leave the step as it was, with saved request ids kept and polled. The job stays running and marked offline, retries after 5, 10, 20 and 40 seconds and then every minute, and the card says "Mất kết nối, sẽ thử lại" with "Thử lại" to try now. `HYAN_FIXTURE_OFFLINE=<seconds>` simulates an outage.
- **Subtitles go on last** and clips are kept without them, so restyling later is free.
- Scripts committed before jobs existed show as not started; nothing starts by itself on upgrade.

## Video tab

A card per script. What it is sits on the left, what can be done on the right: the title, and under it one muted line with the status and the cost; top right the one button that applies, and under it the quiet actions ("Bản trước", "Sửa kịch bản", "Sửa video"). The cost is not beside the button, where it read as that button's price. "▶ Lời thoại" appears when the voice is ready. While a video is being staged the card says "Đang lên dàn dựng". Finished: the player beside its shots, and "Tải xuống". Every word is a state, a number or an action; no guidance text.

**Time per step (23 November 2026; built, not yet seen in the app).** Each job keeps the seconds of work per phase (`phaseSec`: plan, voice, stills, speech, shots, assembly), counted only while the job is running: waiting for another video, the pause, stopped, failed and the app being closed add nothing (`src/shared/phase-clock.ts`, applied wherever the runner saves a job). While a video is made each step shows its time and the current one counts up, and the status line leads with the total ("2:10 · còn ~3 phút"). A finished video shows a clock with the total; hovering it lists the steps. A fix or another quality version adds to the same totals. The staging done before "Tạo video" is not counted, and videos made earlier show nothing.

**A picture full size (23 November 2026; built, not yet seen in the app).** Every shot tile with a picture has "Xem ảnh" on hover or focus; it opens the video's pictures in the full-size viewer (← →, zoom), each shown once and labelled with the shots that use it.

## The video model speaks (20 November 2026; the only way since 25 November)

**Status:** run live behind a developer switch before 25 November (1:12 videos and a character changing accent were seen); the switch is gone and this is every new video.

The video model turns each speaking still, the speaker's voice sample and a text prompt into a clip, and the dialogue track and the timeline are taken from those clips. Code: `src/shared/speech.ts`, `src/shared/video-models.ts`, `speechPrompt` in `src/shared/prompt-text.ts`, `src/shared/voice-step.ts`, `src/main/video/speech.ts`.

- **Voices step** (25 November 2026): when the video starts it records the voice text and sample file of each character who still speaks and keeps them; missing samples are made alongside the stills behind the same first hold (marked, attached after a restart, stopped and resumed like other steps, 10-minute deadline, one automatic retry, then the video fails and "Giữ để tạo tiếp" retries). A segment is never sent before its speaker's sample exists. Cost group "Giọng nhân vật". Details: [voices](VOICE_DECISIONS.md).

- **Speech segments** (pure, `speechSegments`): each `speaker` shot starts a segment; the other shots of the line (wide, detail, listener, product, motion) belong to the segment before them, or to the first one when they come before any speaker shot. A line without a speaker shot gets one segment from a picture of its speaker (else the master), whose video is never shown. A segment's text is its shots' words joined; its hold is its last shot's.
- **Model:** the job's video model. A segment asks for its estimated speaking time plus its hold plus 1 s (2 s until 26 November 2026: on a 13-clip video that asked 67 s of clips for 27 s of planned speech, and the model spoke 2.75 syllables a second to fill them, so a 34 s script became a 55 s video), rounded up to whole seconds (speaking time is syllables ÷ the job's pace since 26 November 2026: the video model stretches speech to fill the clip it is asked for, so the clip length is the tempo control, and the prompt names the pace; the pace is fixed once a segment is sent, and jobs made before keep 2.7 syllables a second, see [script board](SCRIPT_BOARD_DECISIONS.md)); a segment too long for it goes to the other model.
- **Prompt** (code only): who speaks (the main person when the picture has several; the others silent and still), that the scene, people, clothes, light and framing are exactly those of Image 1 and the clip opens on it, the motion the plan wrote for the shot, `They say: "<words>"` with a sound at the end of the line removed from the quote and described after it ("then they laugh softly"), that they say exactly these words once with no other word and then stay silent with the mouth closed until the clip ends (26 November 2026, not yet tried live), that the speaker's voice is the one heard in Audio 1 (same timbre, pitch, age and accent; Audio 1 is only a sample whose words are never repeated), the character's own voice text quoted as the description of that voice, the seat's background motion, "the natural quiet sound of the room; no music", faithfulness, and `CLEAN_FOOTAGE`.
- **Segments are steps like sounds and clips:** in `job.json` (`segments`), marked before sending and the mark in `requests.jsonl` (purpose `video-speech`, with model and length), request id saved on acceptance, attached after a restart, polled, 90-minute deadline, one automatic retry then "Thử lại" on the segment's speaker shot, network trouble handled as for clips, stop and resume as for clips, cost recorded in `usage.jsonl`. Every segment whose still exists is sent at once.
- **Each returned clip** goes through the lettering check with the same limits; a rejected one is set aside (`speech-<id>-rejected-N.mp4`) and redone on the other video model; the segment fails when both were rejected. Speech is then found with FFmpeg `silencedetect` (0.3 s) at a threshold set by the clip's own background: the 20th percentile of its per-frame RMS level plus 16 dB, never below −35 dB nor above −20 dB. Clean clips stay at −35 dB; a clip with a stream or room sound under the speech is listened to above that sound (on 26 Nov a stream under a good clip was never below −35 dB, so it was judged cut off and remade twice). The bounds found are stored and the kept clip is cut there. **Cut off** (since 26 November 2026) means the voice is still sounding in the clip's last 0.08 s, measured on the per-frame levels when the detector found no ending: the video model paces the line to fill the clip, so a complete line normally ends 0.1 to 0.5 s before the clip does, and the earlier rule (less than 0.3 s of quiet at the end) threw away 12 clips of one 13-clip video that all held the full line. A cut-off clip is set aside (`speech-<id>-cut-N.mp4`) as one failed attempt and asked again longer (next allowed length, or 2 s more) once; a cut-off on the retry keeps the clip, with the scene note "Lời nói có thể bị cắt ở cuối, hãy nghe lại cảnh này" (a cut-off never fails a scene). A failed segment tried again by hand ("Thử lại", "Giữ để tạo tiếp") starts from its normal length, since a longer clip only slows the speech and, on short lines, makes the model invent words. Not checked: whether the words spoken are the line's words (needs speech-to-text). A set-aside clip's cost moves to "Làm lại".
- **Timeline:** when every segment is done, each segment's speech is cut with 0.15 s before and after, levelled to −16 LUFS, and joined in line order into the job's dialogue file; `lineSpans`, `dialogueSec` and a per-segment character timing (used only to split shots, so a cut between two segments falls between their words) come from the joined track. The holds are not in that file: they are added after it (`withHolds`, `insertSilence`), so they are counted once; the clip was asked long enough to cover its hold. Then placement, sounds, mix, subtitles (spread across each line by length) and assembly. Speaker shots are local steps: cut from their segment's clip at their offset in it (frozen first or last frame if the shot reaches past the clip), scaled to the output size, no cost of their own.
- **Order:** plan, stills with the missing voice samples beside them, speech segments, timeline, the other shots and sounds, assembly.
- **Prices:** the cost table has no voice row; segments are "Đoạn N · Nói" in the "Cảnh nói" group (estimates saved as "Khẩu hình", "Lời nói" or "Cảnh nói (có tiếng)" show under it, `src/shared/cost/group-names.ts`). Everything the cost code knows about speech is in `src/shared/cost/speech-cost.ts`, registered in `src/shared/cost/parts.ts`.
- **Fixes:** remaking a still no segment starts from, a wide, detail or product shot, or a moving shot works. Anything that would change the speech is refused once speech exists ("Chưa sửa được phần lời nói của video").
- **Fixture mode:** a segment's clip is the still with a stretch of the fixture dialogue starting at 0.4 s and lasting the estimated speaking time, in a clip of the asked length. `HYAN_FIXTURE_CUTOFF=3x1` makes the segment whose speaker shot is shot 3 run its speech to the end once; `HYAN_FIXTURE_FAIL` and `HYAN_FIXTURE_CAPTIONS` apply to a segment by its speaker shot's number.

Unverified: whether either model holds the voice from the sample across clips, Vietnamese quality of the voice-making model, and Wan 3.0's first frame (no start-frame input on its reference endpoint). Not built: music; remaking a speaker shot, a segment or a line; the H3 Max overrun is not measured or trimmed beyond what speech detection does; the estimate before a plan still counts generated pictures for a series without a place.

## Fixture mode

`pnpm dev` simulates every provider step in a few seconds with sample media, so the whole flow can be driven for free. `HYAN_FIXTURE_FAIL="2,4x2"` makes shot 2 fail once and shot 4 twice. Real requests need `HYAN_PROVIDER_MODE=live`.

## Known risks

- The instant between fal accepting a request and the app logging it is not covered: a lost reply could still be paid twice. Rare; check the usage log after live runs.
- Whether a failed shot is billed by the provider is unverified; the app assumes not.
- Very short lines (one or two words) may be shorter than the speaking model accepts. Untested.
- The first live run with no cap on clips is still to be watched: total time from the first clip to assembly, and any clip that stays rendering for long.

## Not built

**Sound beyond room tone (found in the second user test, 21 November 2026; nothing decided).** Today a video gets one room sound, mixed 22 dB under the voice, and a spot sound only on shots where nobody speaks: the two test videos had one spot sound each. There is no sound under a speaking shot, none at a cut, none for the mood of a moment, and no music, by rule. Candidates, each needing a listening test: a louder room sound, action sounds under speaking shots, short sounds at scene changes and at a turn in mood, a music bed per series (needs a source and a decision on rights).

Several videos in parallel; generation while the app is closed; bundling FFmpeg.

**Caption styles (later, a likely differentiator):** a set of styles with size and position, chosen per series, building on today's word-by-word highlight. Drawn by the app itself, not a provider's caption service. Start by testing fonts against hard Vietnamese syllables (stacked accents such as "ễ", "ặ"): most display fonts used by caption tools do not cover them.
